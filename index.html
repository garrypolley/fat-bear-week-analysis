How busy vs that year’s opener?

How to read: Not raw vote counts. Each year is scaled so its own opening-day average pair total = 100. Later stages are “% of that opener.” Horizontal guide = same as opener. Above 100 = busier than day 1; below = quieter.

Y-axis: index (opener = 100). X-axis: stage of the week (aligned across years, not the same calendar dates).

index = 100 × (day avg ÷ opening-day avg) · Source: data/*_matchups.csv

Year-over-year: same stage vs prior year

How to read: Different question. Each bar = this year’s avg pair ÷ last year’s same stage. Guide at 100 = flat YoY. 130 ≈ 30% busier than prior year; 70 ≈ 30% quieter. No 2022 series (nothing to compare against).

2025 Mid vs 2024 Mid (~255) looks huge mainly because 2024 Mid was an unusually quiet trough (~50k), not because 2025 Mid was absurdly high (~128k).

Same-day missing votes

Gap = max pair − min pair that day, as % of max. High gap can mean poll abandonment. Alone ≠ stuffing. 2022 fraud was a short-time spam burst; cleaned archive does not show the intermediate spike.

Only multi-poll main-bracket days. Dashed line = 10% flag. Sole day above 10% in this set: 2026-09-23 (four polls).

Fraud vs noise

Confirmed (2022)

Organizers caught ~9k votes in a short window for Holly, discarded them, 747 advanced. Public Wayback Results = official after cleanup.

Not automatically fraud

Midweek turnout dips (common every year). Same-day gaps under ~10% on two-poll days. Finals rebound. 2026 opening 16% gap fits four-poll abandonment.
